Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about how Rebrand works.
What is Rebrand?
Rebrand turns any property listing into a polished, branded presentation you can share with clients — under your own name, photo and contact details, not the original portal's.
How does it work?
Paste a listing URL. Rebrand imports the title, price, description, key facts and photos, lets you review and edit everything, then generates a shareable presentation page you can send directly to a client.
Does Rebrand use AI?
Yes. The Enhance step uses AI to tidy up the title and description, detect features, and read details that a simple page scrape can miss. You always see and can edit what it produces before publishing.
Do I need permission to use the listing's text and photos?
Yes. Rebrand imports publicly available content to save you time, but it does not verify ownership, copyright or licensing of anything it imports. You're responsible for having the right to use the content you publish — for example because you're the listing agent, or you have the original agent's permission. See our Terms of Service for details.
Which sites can I import from?
Most real estate listing pages work, including ones that load their photo galleries dynamically. Results are usually best when you paste the link from the listing agent's own website rather than a portal like Idealista, which can block or limit automated access.
What if a page's images don't import properly?
Some sites use techniques that make photos harder to detect automatically. Rebrand has several fallback methods it tries automatically. If it still comes up short, you can add a gallery URL manually, or add photos yourself.
Can I use my own branding?
Yes. In Settings you can upload your logo, set your brand colours, and add your name, agency, phone, email and photo — all of it is saved and reused automatically on every listing you generate.
Can I install Rebrand on my phone like an app?
Yes. Open Rebrand in Safari, tap the share icon, then "Add to Home Screen." It'll appear with its own icon and open full-screen, without Safari's address bar.
Where is my data stored?
Your contact card and branding preferences are stored locally on your device. Generated presentations are stored securely so they can be shared with a link. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Who can see a presentation I generate?
Anyone with the link. Presentations aren't listed publicly or indexed for search — treat the link the way you would any other document you'd share directly with a client.
